About DonateConnect

Making donations more transparent, local and trustworthy.

Our Mission

DonateConnect aims to connect people who want to donate with verified NGOs working in their local communities.

Our main focus is transparency. Donors should be able to understand where their donation went and what impact it created.

Our Process

Donor → NGO → Beneficiary → Proof

Every donation gets a unique Donation ID for tracking.
